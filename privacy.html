Privacy Policy

Sudoku Studio · Last updated 2 September 2026

Sudoku Studio is a sudoku game for iPhone. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, where it goes, and how to remove it. It covers the app only.

What the app collects

DataWhyWhere it is stored
Email address To create and sign you in to your account Firebase Authentication
Account identifier To tell your account apart from other accounts Firebase Authentication
Display name Shown on your profile. Optional. Firebase Authentication
Game progress and statistics To continue your puzzles and track your record Your device only — never uploaded

That is the complete list. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing is shared with data brokers, and nothing is used to build a profile of you or to follow you across other apps or websites.

Your game data stays on your device

Puzzle progress, completion times, streaks and settings are stored locally on your iPhone and are not sent to any server. They are not backed up to our systems and are not synced between devices. If you delete the app, that data is gone.

Signing in with Google

If you sign in with Google, Google confirms your identity and passes your email address and a Google account identifier to the app. The app never sees your Google password. Google's own handling of that sign-in is covered by their privacy policy.

Sign in with Apple

If you sign in with Apple you may choose Hide My Email. Apple then gives the app a private relay address instead of your real one, and the app never sees your actual email address. This is fully supported — you can use the app normally.

Face ID

If you turn on biometric unlock, iOS performs the Face ID check itself and only tells the app whether it succeeded. The app never receives your face data. Credentials for this feature are held in the iOS Keychain on your device.

Who processes your data

Account sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, a service operated by Google. Your email address and account identifier are stored on their servers on our behalf. See the Firebase privacy documentation for how they handle it.

You can sign in three ways — with Apple, with Google, or with an email address and password. Whichever you choose, the same small set of data above is what the app ends up holding.

Sudoku Studio does not sell your data, and does not share it with anyone else.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app, at any time, without contacting anyone: Profile → Account deletion.

Deleting your account:

  • removes your account and email address from Firebase Authentication
  • if you signed in with Apple, revokes the app's token, so Sudoku Studio disappears from Settings → Sign in with Apple on your device
  • clears your saved progress and any stored credentials from the device

This is immediate and cannot be undone. Signing out also clears saved progress from the device.

Children

Sudoku Studio is not directed at children under 13, and the app does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes
