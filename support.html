Sudoku Studio Support

Help, bug reports, and how to reach a human.

Get in touch

Email support@sudokustudiogame.com. To get a useful answer faster, include:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version
  • the app version, shown at the bottom of the Profile screen
  • what you did, what happened, and what you expected instead

Common questions

Does my progress sync between my iPhone and iPad?

No. Progress is stored on each device separately. Signing in saves your progress on that device; it does not copy it to another one.

I signed out and my progress is gone.

Signing out clears saved progress from the device by design, so a shared iPhone does not leave your game behind for the next person. It cannot be recovered.

I used "Hide My Email" and never got the verification email.

You do not need one. Apple has already verified your address, so Sign in with Apple takes you straight into the app. Verification emails apply only to accounts created with an email and password.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Account deletion. It removes your account immediately, revokes the app's Sign in with Apple access, and clears your data from the device. It cannot be undone.

It says I need to sign in again before deleting my account.

For security, deleting an account requires a recent sign-in. Sign out, sign back in, and try again.

Face ID isn't working.

Check that Face ID is enabled for Sudoku Studio in iOS Settings → Face ID & Passcode → Other Apps. You can always sign in with your email and password instead.
